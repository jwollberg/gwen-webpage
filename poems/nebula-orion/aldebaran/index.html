aldebaran

sometimes when the water is warm and the heavens can be seen, and the nighttime sky is upon me… i can see another dimension, another world, a peaceful place where faeries go. a secret life, beyond your dreams, with crystal-like palaces and beautiful scenes. with unicorns grazing and galloping too, across sparkling waters in the eye of the bull…

a following taurus girl, not mystified, by her surroundings nor her life. she plays the harp of enchanted dreams and hopes someday that she can be on aldebaran.
