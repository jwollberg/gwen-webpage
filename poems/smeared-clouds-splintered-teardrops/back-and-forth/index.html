smeared clouds & splintered teardrops

back and forth

a broken pendant tossed in an overflowing junk drawer i feel worthless, in a meaningless life

a carousel spinning around i circle repeatedly

when your god is presented to me these thoughts are abandoned your god charms me when i look up he’s gone

a swing  on a playground i go back and forth

a child drawn to a candy store i am enticed, but walk away each time
