Dark poetry & literature

Gwenevere Ivy Greenwood is an American writer of dark poetry and literary fiction. Deeply inspired by the natural world, her work blends symbolism, psychological depth, and atmospheric storytelling to explore grief, memory, trauma, healing, and the quiet resilience of the human spirit. Through recurring images drawn from nature, dreams, and myth, she seeks beauty and meaning within darkness.

Content warning This website contains fictional works and poetry that explore dark and potentially disturbing themes, including suicidal ideation, self-harm, grief, and death. Some readers may find the material distressing or offensive.
